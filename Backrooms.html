THE BACKROOMS

You are in your suburban home. Everything feels normal... until it isn't. Find the glitching yellow wall anomaly in your house to clip out of reality. Once inside, find the glowing green exit in each level to descend through all 50 levels, falling through the sky between each set of ten. Watch your sanity and your health. Blue bottles restore health, white restore sanity.

WASD move | Mouse (click to lock) or arrow keys look | SHIFT sprint | F flashlight | SPACE / C swim up / down (Level 7)

MULTIPLAYER: friends who enter the same room code get the same maps and appear as hazmat-suited survivors on your level. Leave blank to play solo.
